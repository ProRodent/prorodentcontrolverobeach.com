Every service below follows the same underlying sequence: inspect first to confirm the species and find the entry point, treat to clear out what is active, seal the opening so the next animal cannot repeat the route, then follow up to confirm it held. Some jobs need the full sequence, and some need just one piece of it, which is why each service has its own page rather than being bundled into a single generic offering.

If you are not sure which service matches what you are dealing with, describe it on the call, sounds, droppings, a smell, visible damage, and we will point you toward the right one, or start with a standalone rodent inspection and estimates if the picture is not clear yet.

Questions about our services

Which service should I start with if I am not sure what is going on?

A rodent inspection and estimates visit is a reasonable starting point whenever the entry point or species is not obvious yet.

Do I need every service, or just one?

Most calls need a combination, inspection plus trapping and sealing at minimum, but the exact scope depends entirely on what the inspection finds.

Are these services available for businesses too?

Yes, our commercial rodent control service covers shops, restaurants and offices with the same underlying approach.
