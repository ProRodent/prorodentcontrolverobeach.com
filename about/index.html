Who picks up the phone.

There is no booking widget or contact form here. Every job starts with a phone call, answered around the clock, where you describe what is going on: a sound at night, droppings in a cupboard, a chewed wire. That conversation is what a technician uses to plan the first visit, and a same-day slot is offered whenever the day's schedule has room for one.

The four steps behind a visit.

A property in Vero Beach, FL gets the same sequence whether the complaint is a mouse in the pantry or rats along a canal bank. Inspect first to find the entry point and confirm the species, treat to clear out what is active, seal the opening so the next animal cannot repeat the route, then come back to confirm it held. Twelve individual services sit under that sequence, from a standalone rodent inspection through mouse control, rat control, cleanup and structural repair, all listed on our services page.

A visit, start to finish.

Most calls open with a description over the phone, sounds, droppings, damage, or an animal actually seen, and that leads to an on-site inspection to pin down the species and locate where they are getting in. What happens after that depends entirely on what the inspection turns up: trapping, sealing work, attic or crawl space cleanup, or repairing chewed material, each one explained before it starts rather than bundled into a surprise.
