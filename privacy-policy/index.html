What we collect.

When you call or email us, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, phone number, address, and details about the rodent issue you are reporting. This site does not use a sign-up form to collect visitor emails; any email address we hold comes from a message you sent us directly.

How we use it.

Information you provide is used to schedule a visit, plan the technician's approach, and keep a record of what was found and treated at your property for future reference. We do not sell contact information to third parties.

Website analytics.

Like most websites, this site may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use it, which does not identify you personally and is separate from the contact information collected during an actual call or email.

Contacting us about your information.

If you have questions about information we hold about you, contact us using the details on our contact page.
